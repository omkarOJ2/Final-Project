Writing Tips
  • Start with a strong, attention-grabbing headline
  • Use the inverted pyramid structure (most important first)
  • Keep paragraphs short and focused (3-4 sentences)
  • Use active voice and strong verbs
  • Include quotes for credibility and human interest
  • End with a strong conclusion or call-to-action
